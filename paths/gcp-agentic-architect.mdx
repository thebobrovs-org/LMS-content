This path prepares you to design an agentic system on Google Cloud end to end and
to defend each choice: low-code or code, which model, how the agent reaches
enterprise data and tools, how agents coordinate, how the system is evaluated
before and after launch, where it runs, and how its identity, access and
guardrails are set.

It runs **chapter by chapter**, with the lessons in order inside each one.
Foundations comes first. Chapters 2 to 6 follow the exam's five sections (low-code
agents, coding agents, custom agents, evaluation and deployment, security and
governance), and each starts simple and ends with running the thing in production.
The capstone comes last.

Lessons are added one at a time. A chapter without a published lesson yet stays
hidden until its first lesson ships.
