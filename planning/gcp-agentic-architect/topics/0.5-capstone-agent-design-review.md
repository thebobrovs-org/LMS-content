# 0.5 Capstone: design and review an agentic system

**Topic id:** `agentic-ai/capstone-agent-design-review` · **Level:** 400 · **Difficulty:** advanced · **Est:** 45 min
**Track:** Core · **Exam objective:** all sections · **Persona:** `personas/agentic-ai.md`
**Status:** in review

## Why / What / How
- **Why:** The exam's scenarios and its two hands-on labs test whether you can combine every choice. This topic rehearses that end to end.
- **What:** One realistic brief (a customer-service agent with order lookups, refunds and an internal knowledge base) designed across all five sections, then a design review that hunts for the classic mistakes.
- **How:** `<Steps>` walk the design: build approach, model, tools and knowledge, orchestration, evaluation plan, runtime, identity, auth, gateway and guardrails. Then the learner reviews a flawed design and finds its five defects.

## Learning objectives
After this topic the learner can (ids become the lesson's `objectives`):
- `end-to-end-design`: Produce an end-to-end design that names and justifies each choice.
- `design-review`: Find the defects in a flawed agent design and fix each.

## Must-learn (keep nothing else but this)
1. Every choice traces to a requirement in the brief.
2. Evaluation, identity and guardrails are designed in, not added at the end.

## Key terms (glossary)
- `design review`: A structured check of a design against requirements and known failure modes.
