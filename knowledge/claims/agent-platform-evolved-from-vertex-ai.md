**Claim.** Google describes Gemini Enterprise Agent Platform (Agent Platform for short) as the evolution of Vertex AI, announced at Google Cloud Next on 22 April 2026; Google says Vertex AI services and roadmap are delivered through Agent Platform from then on. ADK, Agent Runtime, RAG Engine, the Gen AI evaluation service and the governance products (Agent Identity, Agent Gateway, Agent Registry) are documented under it.

**Limits.** "Evolution" is Google's word, not a one-to-one rename of every Vertex AI service. Gemini Enterprise, the employee-facing agent product, is a separate product despite the shared prefix.
